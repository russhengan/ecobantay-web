🗺️ How to Save a Route
  1. Click Manage Routes.
  2. Enter Route Name at (optional) Barangay.
  3. Kung wala kang coordinates, kumuha muna sa Google Maps.
  4. Lagyan ng street names o lat,lng per line.
  5. Click Save Route.
Step 1 — Open Manage Routes
Step 2 — Fill Route Name & Barangay
Tip: Open Google Maps, right-click your target location, choose "What's here?", then copy the latitude and longitude (e.g. 14.7012, 120.9611) and paste it into the Waypoints box.
Step 3 — Enter Waypoints
💡 Note: The first line is for the starting coordinate.
After typing it, press Enter, and the second line is for the ending coordinate of the route.
Step 4 — Save Route
Step 5 — Verify in Existing Routes
📅 How to Create a Collection Schedule
  1. Click Create Schedule.
  2. Fill Barangay/Street at piliin ang Shift.
  3. Pumili ng Route at piliin ang Days.
  4. Click Create.
Step 1 — Open Create Schedule
Click the Create Schedule button on the main Scheduling page.
Step 2 — Fill Details
Enter the Barangay and Street, then select the preferred Shift.
Step 3 — Pick Route & Days
Choose the appropriate Route from the dropdown and select the collection Days.
Step 4 — Create
Review your details and click Create to save the schedule.
